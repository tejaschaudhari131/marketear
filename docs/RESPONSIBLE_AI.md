# Responsible AI, data and safety — pass/fail account for Market Ear v2

Label key: FACT = verified in this repo or a cited source. INFERENCE = reasoning. UNKNOWN = not verified.
Scope: the prototype is a clerk-confirmed price check. It does not give advice, does not decide anything, and sends nothing by itself.

| # | Question the brief asks | Our answer | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fail-safe: does it say "not sure - ask a person" instead of guessing? | Yes. Four separate triggers (FACT, `src/marketear.py`): crop, market or price not decodable; another crop or price fits the audio within 2.0 log-prob points; the closed-vocabulary reading is more than 25 log-prob points less likely than the free transcript (audio does not fit, e.g. coffee or weather news); WFP has no recent data for that market, crop and unit (shown as "No reference", not as a guess). Measured on 6 out-of-vocabulary clips: all 6 rejected, but thresholds were tuned on the same small set, so treat as UNVERIFIED beyond that. Known gap (FACT): the margin rule did NOT catch confusions between crops that sound alike (wrong clips had margins 2.0 to 16.6), so these still depend on the human check. | PASS with a stated limit |
| 2 | Human in the loop | The clerk sees the decoded text, the free transcript, the extracted fields, the WFP rows used and the reason for any abstention, and presses Confirm or Reject. The demo has no send function at all (FACT: `ui/index.html`). In deployment an SMS is prepared for the clerk to send; the model never sends. | PASS |
| 3 | No harmful advice | The tool states what the bulletin said and a public reference range. It never says sell, hold, or accept. The word "above/below the range" is a comparison, not a recommendation. | PASS |
| 4 | Where the data sits | Audio is processed on the clerk's own computer; the model runs offline on CPU (no cloud call). The server deletes the uploaded audio and its converted copy at the end of each request (FACT: `ui/server.py`). No transcripts or results are written to disk by the demo. WFP tables are public files shipped with the tool. | PASS |
| 5 | Who can read it | Only the person at the clerk's computer. Radio bulletins are public broadcasts; WFP prices are public (CC BY-IGO). No farmer names, phone numbers or locations of farms are collected. | PASS |
| 6 | Lost or shared phone | The farmer's phone only holds an SMS with a public market price and a public reference range, with no name. Losing or sharing it exposes nothing the broadcast did not already say. The clerk's computer holds no record of members. INFERENCE: if a co-op later adds a member list for SMS delivery, that list becomes personal data and needs its own consent and access rules; it is out of scope here. | PASS (scope stated) |
| 7 | Consent | No personal data is collected from farmers. A recording of a person's voice (the build team's own test voice) is used only with that person's consent and stays on the build box. | PASS |
| 8 | Bias and coverage | FACT: the recogniser was trained on read Swahili (Common Voice 11); we tested on FLEURS Kenyan read speech (25.0% WER on 30 clips), 40 RFI news sentences, synthetic voices and one speaker's script. UNKNOWN: accuracy for women, older speakers, Ugandan or coastal accents, spontaneous radio speech, music behind speech. Swahili only; Luganda and other languages are not supported and will be rejected by the closed vocabulary. The system is not tested on any real market-price broadcast because none exists in open data. | PASS only if stated in the video and README (it is) |
| 9 | Data gaps stated | WFP: no coffee; per-kg Tanzanian series end 2022-12; Kenyan highland markets thin after mid-2025; no farm-gate prices. See `docs/DATA_TABLE.md`. | PASS |
| 10 | No overclaiming of impact | FACT: Aker and Fafchamps (2015, World Bank Economic Review 29(2):262-292) found in Niger that mobile phone coverage reduced cowpea price dispersion by about 6% but had no effect on producer price levels and no dispersion effect for storable millet and sorghum. So information alone does not guarantee better prices. We claim only a faster, checkable reference price; we have NOT measured any effect on what a farmer is paid. | PASS |
| 11 | Safety of radio use | FM reception is receive-only; the tool never transmits. UNKNOWN: whether recording or relaying a station's broadcast needs a licence or the station's permission in each country; a deployment must ask the station. | PASS with an open item |
| 12 | Third-party data and model hygiene | The model is loaded from a safetensors file; third-party `.npy` IQ files were loaded only after the pickle opcodes were checked (FACT, `ideation2_scripts/demod.py` note). MMS text-to-speech (CC BY-NC) is used only to make test audio and is not part of the product. | PASS |
| 13 | Labelling of simulation | Every radio-channel result is labelled SIMULATED (software FM model, no multipath or fading, mono, nominal CNR). Synthetic TTS speech is labelled SYNTHETIC. The demo screen repeats the label whenever the channel is on. | PASS |
| 14 | Language checks | The Swahili strings (the "not sure" phrase, the SMS draft, the recording script) were written by a non-native builder and are NOT checked by a native speaker. OPEN: do not present them as verified. | OPEN |

## What would make this fail
- Presenting a simulated-channel number as a real-radio result.
- Letting the tool send an SMS without a person pressing confirm.
- Showing a WFP range for a market, crop or unit where the table has no recent data.
- Saying that the price check raises what farmers are paid.
